## Search Central Live is coming to Bogota and Ciudad de México

Tuesday, September 8, 2026

Leer en español

We're very excited to announce that for the first time ever Search Central Live is coming to Bogota, Colombia on October 19.

But wait, there's more! The week before that, on October 15, we'll also come back to Ciudad de México. We saw and heard you all at our previous editions of Search Central Live in Mexico. Together with our past events in Argentina and Brazil, we want to meet as many of you as possible in Latin America. We're inviting you all: SEOs, publishers, creators, digital marketers, and website owners to join us for a mix of presentations on Google Search, Google Discover, News, and more!

While some of the presentations will be held in English, there will be Spanish translations available as well. As this is a live event with limited access, we encourage you to register for a chance to get one of the free seats as early as possible.

For Ciudad de México: The event will take place on October 15, between 8:30am and 1:30pm. Find out more about speakers and agenda on the event website . Registration will be open until Sep 16, 2026, and confirmations will be sent on Sep 21, 2026, including the address and other event details.

Apply for an invitation to Ciudad de México

For Bogota: The event will take place on October 19 between 2:30pm and 6:30pm. Find out more about speakers and agenda on the event website . Registration will be open until Sep 16, 2026, and confirmations will be sent on Sep 21, 2026, including the address and other event details.

Apply for an invitation to Bogota

We're looking forward to meeting in person and chatting about Google Search, Discover, and News.

Posted by Martin Splitt , Search relations, and Alejandra Brambila , Google News Partnerships Mexico
