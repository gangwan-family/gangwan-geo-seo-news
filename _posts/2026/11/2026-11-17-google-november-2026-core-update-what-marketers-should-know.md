## Google November 2026 core update: What marketers should know

Author : Cecilia Meis

3 min read

November 17, 2026

According to the Google Search Status Dashboard , Google started rolling out its November 2026 core update on November 10. The update applies globally across all languages, and Google says the rollout could take up to two weeks.

Google Search Central also announced the update on LinkedIn , calling it a regular update meant to better surface relevant, satisfying content from all types of sites.

This is Google's third core update of 2026, following the March update (March 27 to April 8) and the May update (May 21 to June 2). It's also the eighth ranking update listed on the dashboard this year, about a month after the September 2026 spam update finished on October 8.

Google hasn't shared what changed, but the update didn't arrive out of nowhere. Search Engine Roundtable reported fresh ranking volatility on November 9, the day before Google confirmed the rollout.

### Why the November core update matters for marketers

If the rollout uses its full two-week window, it could run until November 24, three days before Black Friday. It could also run longer. This year's March and May core updates each finished in about 12 days, but the November 2024 core update took nearly 24 days. The November 2023 update took almost 26. A rollout that long would run through Cyber Monday. Either way, ranking movement arrives on top of peak holiday demand.

Google's advice is to wait at least a full week after the rollout finishes before comparing Search Console performance data with a week before the update began. But with Black Friday falling so close to the rollout, two factors could make those comparisons harder to interpret:

- Seasonality : Retail sites will be comparing holiday traffic to an ordinary week, making it harder to separate changes in search demand from changes in rankings

- Data reliability : Search Engine Roundtable noted that Search Console's performance report has been off for many sites in recent weeks, and Google hasn't commented on the issue

Google hasn't specified how the update may affect AI Overviews or AI Mode. Marketers monitoring AI search visibility should watch for changes in citations alongside organic rankings as the rollout progresses. If you check Search Console's generative AI report, Google confirmed a logging error lowered impressions from November 9 to 11, which overlaps the first two days of the rollout.

### How to track the November core update's impact

Review where your key pages and keywords stood before November 10, then compare their performance with the wider market as the rollout progresses. Start with Position Tracking . If you already have a Position Tracking campaign, review your pre-update rankings. Otherwise, set one up now to monitor changes throughout the remaining rollout. That helps you track which keywords and pages gain or lose ground as the rollout continues. A new campaign only collects data from the day you create it, so use Search Console for your pre-update baseline.

Then use Semrush Sensor to see whether your swings match the broader SERP volatility. Sensor scores daily Google volatility from 0 to 10 by country, device, and category. Connect your Position Tracking campaign to get a personal volatility score for your own keywords. Rank trackers, Semrush included, are also filtering out "poisoned" results that Google returns to some automated requests. If a keyword that has nothing to do with your page suddenly swings, check it against Search Console before taking action.

For enterprise teams, a core update can move rankings across multiple brands, regions, and thousands of URLs at once. Semrush Enterprise tracks rankings and organic visibility at that scale.

Whatever your setup, avoid major site changes until the rollout finishes. Once rankings settle, compare the pages that gained or lost visibility and look for patterns before deciding what needs attention.
