## LVMH’s Media Division announces strategic partnership with OpenAI

This news was originally shared by LVMH’s Media Division and can be read here ⁠ (opens in a new window) .

Paris, 16 November 2026 —LVMH Group’s Media Division and OpenAI announce the signing of a strategic agreement to support new uses of artificial intelligence across all the division’s publications and make their journalism more accessible through ChatGPT. The agreement includes the deployment of OpenAI tools and ChatGPT Enterprise to teams across the media division, enabling them to develop AI applications tailored to their day-to-day work.

Their shared ambition is to empower teams across the media division to experiment with and develop practical uses of AI that support journalism and high-quality reporting.

Artificial intelligence is a powerful tool that, when put to work in newsrooms, enables journalists to focus on the core of their profession: investigating, analysing and writing. Teams will be able to explore applications such as document research, document comparison and preliminary translation. The goal is to enable them to devote more time to investigative reporting, analysis and reporting in the field.

In addition, in an increasingly crowded information landscape, personalisation has become a major priority. Artificial intelligence is an essential tool for developing and delivering products and services that meet the needs of customers, readers and advertisers alike, helping to attract and retain them and grow revenue across the publications.

This partnership is a company-wide initiative that also enhances the presence of content from LVMH Group’s Media Division in ChatGPT, providing summaries and excerpts in response to relevant user questions, identifying their sources and including direct links to the original articles. The partnership will extend across all the division’s publications and support the evolution of roles throughout the organisation: streamlining operations, improving efficiency, and encouraging innovation and creativity to prepare today’s publications for tomorrow. The rollout will be supported by training tailored to everyone’s use cases.

“ LVMH’s Media Division (Les Echos, Le Parisien, Challenges, Paris Match, L’Opinion, Investir, L’Agefi and others) and OpenAI are entering into a strategic partnership. This agreement will position our publications at the forefront of the media industry. Integrating AI will become an everyday asset for all our teams. We are delighted to embark on this journey with a partner whose expertise and reputation are recognised worldwide. ”

—Michèle Benbunan, CEO of LVMH Group’s Media Division

“ Quality journalism depends on the people who investigate, verify facts and help audiences make sense of information. We want to help put AI to work for everyone across LVMH’s Media Division, giving teams tools to explore their stories and develop new uses of AI. Our ambition is to help them spend more time on what makes their journalism valuable, while respecting their editorial independence. This partnership will also give ChatGPT users access to high-quality journalism that keeps pace with the news. ”

—Emmanuel Marill, Managing Director, EMEA at OpenAI

### About LVMH Group’s Media Division

LVMH Group’s media division, Groupe Les Echos-Le Parisien, brings together 17 media brands covering news, finance, culture, heritage, arts and music. With 1,800 employees, including nearly 700 journalists, it reaches more than 27 million people in France every month.

The Group’s media brands: Boursier.com ⁠ (opens in a new window) , Capital Finance, Challenges, Connaissance des Arts, Historia, Investir, La Lettre de l’Expansion, L’Agefi, Le Parisien, Aujourd’hui en France, Les Echos, L’Opinion, medici.tv ⁠ (opens in a new window) , Mezzo, Mieux Vivre Votre Argent, Paris Match, Radio Classique.

The Group’s exhibitions and publishing houses: Citadelles & Mazenod, Fine Arts Paris, In Fine, Salon du Dessin.
