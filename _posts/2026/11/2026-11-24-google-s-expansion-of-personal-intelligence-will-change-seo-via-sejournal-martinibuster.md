## Google’s Expansion Of Personal Intelligence Will Change SEO

Google is bringing together its products and services in a way that could fundamentally change what it means to do SEO.

Google announced the expansion of its Personal Intelligence feature. The expansion unifies many of Google’s disparate products and services into a single personalized search experience. This update represents a profound change to Google’s Search experience and is likely among the most consequential changes to SEO.

Google’s announcement explains the connection to Search:

“The most helpful search experience brings together the richness of the world’s information with insights uniquely relevant to you. Today, Search is getting even more tailored with the ability to connect more of your apps to Personal Intelligence — and we’re introducing more personally relevant responses, so it’s easier to find just what you need.”

Some SEOs objected to characterizing Personal Intelligence as something that is about Search, explaining that Personal Intelligence is about Gemini, AI Mode, and AI Overviews. That view can be justified because Google’s announcement is somewhat ambiguous.

Google’s announcement uses the upper-case word Search sixteen times. That seems to be a clear signal that Personal Intelligence affects Search.

According to Google:

“A more tailored Google Search that’s uniquely yours…

…Get more helpful Search responses…

…Today, Search is getting even more tailored with the ability to connect more of your apps to Personal Intelligence…

…bringing context from your spending, documents, and chats directly to Search.”

The following passage seems to unambiguously paint the rollout as affecting Search:

“Starting to roll out today, you can opt in to securely connect Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, with support for Drive and the Gemini app following in the coming weeks — bringing context from your spending, documents, and chats directly to Search.”

The above passage clearly separates AI Mode from Search. Yet, other passages reference AI Mode in Search, introducing a certain amount of ambiguity. An argument can be made that this does not affect Search, but there’s a strong counter-argument there, too.

### Information Flow Between AI & Search

Google’s announcement ties AI Overviews and AI Mode responses to recent Search contexts, meaning that contextual data from Search can be shared to AIO and AI Mode:

“You’ll also start seeing more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode, tailored to your recent search context.”

And in another passage Google explains that the contextual data from the Gemini App can be shared with AI Mode in Search:

“In the coming weeks you can easily share context across the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search.”

And elsewhere:

“So if you explore an idea in the Gemini app, AI Mode will have that foundation to build on, and vice versa.”

So it looks like the context data flows from Search to AI Overviews and AI Mode as well as from the Gemini App to AI Mode in Search.

The announcement identifies six connected apps:

- Gmail

- Google Calendar

- Google Photos

- Google Wallet

- Google Drive

- Gemini

### Google’s Agentic Layer

But Personal Intelligence is about a whole lot more. It’s about the transformation of disparate services and products into what Google CEO, Sundar Pichai, described in October 2026 as one unified search experience that’s mediated by an agentic layer.

Pichai explained in October :

“I expect our agents to provide that unifying experience across these products. In fact, that agentic layer, rather than try to converge or think about different products and how they come together, I think the agentic layer naturally ends up doing that, right?

Because your agents, they’re running in the cloud, they’re running 24/7, they have your context. And so they can pick up things where you left off.

So you can start a task in some place. You go to the other place. Your agent has that context for you.

So I think the agentic layer offers us a chance to bring a unified product experience better than ever before.”

### Expansion Of Personal Intelligence

Google’s Personal Intelligence was first announced as a paid Gemini App beta feature in January 2026, which was expanded to AI Mode in search about a week later, initially supporting Gmail and Google Photos as an opt-in Search Labs experiment.

In March it was expanded to free tier users in the United States and within a few months it was available in 200 countries and 98 languages.

### What Changed In Personal Intelligence

Google announced the expansion of the range of information AI Mode can draw on through Personal Intelligence so that now it includes Google Wallet data, files stored in Google Drive, and Gemini conversations. Google also announced increased personalization of AI Overviews and AI Mode based on recent search activity.

#### Google Wallet

Opt-in connectivity of Google Wallet to AI Mode is beginning to roll out. This is really big and ties into Sundar Pichai’s vision of Search becoming increasingly focused on helping users accomplish tasks. Google Wallet aggregates data from linked accounts like Gmail and partner apps.

Google Wallet affects travel (like airline tickets), finance, commerce, event tickets, plus sensitive data (in some regions) like drivers license, and health insurance cards. When this feature is enabled, Google Wallet automatically scans incoming emails to import boarding passes, event tickets, loyalty cards, and shipping updates.

Now all of that is connected with Personal Intelligence.

#### Google Drive

Connectivity to AI Mode is coming in the next few weeks. Google announced that connecting Google Drive to Personal Intelligence will enable AI Mode to pull information from across stored Google Drive documents, spreadsheets, and PDFs to provide personalized responses and assist users with projects, planning, and decision-making.

Google explains:

“With Google Drive connected in Personal Intelligence, you can get uniquely helpful responses that take all of that context into account and help you get started, like a personalized preparation plan ahead of tax season.”

### Gemini, Search And Transparency

Personal Intelligence will be sharing Gemini conversational context between the Gemini app and AI Mode within the next few weeks. Search personalization will come in the form of more personally relevant responses based on recent search activity.

Personalization transparency is coming soon. Users will be able to see what personal context AI Mode used to generate a response and correct that information.

### Takeaways

Transformation Of Google Search

Personal Intelligence represents a fundamental change in how Google Search (and/or AI Mode in Search) can respond to users, incorporating knowledge about their activities alongside information from the web.

Unification Of Google’s Products And Services

Previously disparate Google products and services are now increasingly part of a unified, personalized search experience.

Google’s Agentic Layer

The expansion of Personal Intelligence is consistent with Sundar Pichai’s vision of a unified, task-based experience that maintains context across Google’s products.

Sharing Of Contextual Data

The sharing of personal and conversational context across Google’s products enables users to continue activities without having to start over.

Expansion Beyond Information Discovery

The integration of additional data sources enables Google to become increasingly useful for planning, decision-making, and accomplishing tasks.

Increasingly Personalized Search Experiences

Search responses are becoming more specific to individual users, a change that SEOs need to really think about and integrate into how what they do fits into this new multi-dimensional level of Search.

How To Opt Out Of Google Personal Intelligence

Google is giving users greater insight into and control over the personal context used to generate AI Mode responses. Users can opt out or in from Personal Intelligence her e.

### Final Thoughts

Google Search is increasingly an experience that’s as much about what Google knows about the user and what they want to accomplish as it is about what the user is searching for. This is arguably one of the most consequential moments in SEO that I have experienced in my nearly 30 years of experience in SEO.
