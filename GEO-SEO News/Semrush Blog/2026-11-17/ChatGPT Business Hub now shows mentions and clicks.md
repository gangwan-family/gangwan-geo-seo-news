## ChatGPT Business Hub now shows mentions and clicks

Author : Cecilia Meis

4 min read

November 17, 2026

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on November 10. The program's Business Hub lets companies claim their business in ChatGPT, update their information, and see how often ChatGPT mentions them and generates clicks to their website.

ChatGPT is the latest AI platform to report visibility data directly to the businesses it surfaces. SEO consultant Brodie Clark shared the news on X , and highlighted the addition of click and mention data. Until now, marketers have largely relied on prompt testing and referral traffic to understand their ChatGPT visibility.

OpenAI is also launching Business Agents, starting with eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the U.S. These let ChatGPT users open a labeled conversation with a business to ask follow-up questions about its products. These conversations are clearly labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT’s independent answers. Businesses can also sponsor their agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT Ads.

### What the ChatGPT Business Hub shows

The ChatGPT Business Hub shows aggregated data on how people engage with your business in ChatGPT. This includes:

- Mentions : How often ChatGPT references your website or shows your business as a local result. Your brand name alone doesn’t count.

- Website clicks : Recorded clicks on links to your website from ChatGPT

- Discovery topics : The common themes across conversations where ChatGPT cites your website

- Profile views : How many people view your business profile in ChatGPT, which appears as a separate tile in the launch-day dashboard Search Engine Roundtable published

Verified businesses can update key details and add product feeds with pricing and availability. That gives businesses a way to provide more accurate, up-to-date information to ChatGPT.

OpenAI says the insights are aggregated, cover the past 28 days, and appear only once a business has enough conversations or interactions to meet certain thresholds. Businesses can't see individual user conversations.

### How to claim your business in ChatGPT

To claim your business in ChatGPT, find it in Business Hub and verify that you own or represent it:

- Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub

- Search for your business by name and select the matching listing. If it isn’t listed and you run an eligible online business, you may see an option to add it. Local stores and restaurants can only claim an existing U.S. listing.

- Verify ownership through a text or phone call to your main business number, a code sent to your business email, or a DNS record on your domain

- Complete identity verification with Persona. You’ll need it before you can view insights or publish changes, and it adds the blue verified check to your profile.

Shopify merchants can connect their store from Business Hub or the Shopify admin to speed up verification. Existing OpenAI Enterprise customers and advertisers can claim through their OpenAI representative.

Any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim a listing. OpenAI's help doc says availability depends on the business's location and type.

### What the ChatGPT Business Hub means for marketers

For marketers, Business Hub provides a new way to measure ChatGPT visibility using first-party data. With OpenAI reporting 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users , businesses should check whether they're eligible to claim a listing and verify that their information is accurate.

Business Hub covers one platform, in beta. It's also the third platform-specific AI report this year, after Bing in February and Google Search Console in June. Bing’s AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools shows which pages are cited in Copilot and Bing's AI-generated answers, along with the queries associated with those citations. Unlike ChatGPT's Business Hub, Bing's report doesn't include click data. Neither does Google's , which shows impressions only.

Read the mention count carefully. For an online business, a mention counts only when ChatGPT references your website, not when it just names your brand. AI visibility tools, Semrush's included, track brand mentions and citations separately, so expect Business Hub's number to run lower than your mention count and compare it to your citation data instead. Claiming your profile also won't change how often you show up, according to OpenAI's help doc .

The Business Hub also doesn't show which prompts triggered your mentions or how your visibility compares to competitors. And don't expect numbers right away. Barry Schwartz, executive editor of Search Engine Roundtable, verified his business via DNS on launch day and saw no data in the dashboard yet. He also said he's worried about people hijacking business listings, which is another reason to claim yours early.
