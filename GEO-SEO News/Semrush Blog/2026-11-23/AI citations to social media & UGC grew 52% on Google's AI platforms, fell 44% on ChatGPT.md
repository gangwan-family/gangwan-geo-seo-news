## AI citations to social media & UGC grew 52% on Google's AI platforms, fell 44% on ChatGPT

Author : Luke Harsel

8 min read

November 23, 2026

Contributor: Nikola Manojlović

User-generated content (UGC) and social media sites like Reddit and YouTube are showing up more often in Google’s AI answers and less often in ChatGPT’s.

With AI Overviews increasingly common across commercial and branded searches , this matters for marketers. How people talk about your brands on those sites increasingly shapes how AI presents you to searchers.

We wanted to measure the role UGC sites play across different AI search surfaces. We analyzed eight months of Semrush U.S. data, from January through August 2026, covering 18 top UGC and social media domains across Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, and ChatGPT.

We included community and Q&A platforms, video sites, social networks, and publishing platforms such as YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Medium, Pinterest, and Substack.

Below, we break down how citation trends changed by AI platform, which UGC sites gained and lost visibility, and how much AI referral traffic they actually received.

### Key takeaways

- Combined citations to the 18 tracked UGC domains rose 52% across Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Gemini from January to August 2026, while ChatGPT citations fell 44%.

- YouTube led AI Overviews with 45% of tracked UGC citations. Reddit dominated ChatGPT at 52%.

- 17 of the 18 UGC sites increased their monthly AI Overview citations. Quora was the one exception.

- 12 of the 18 UGC sites lost monthly ChatGPT citations, including Medium (-93%), Pinterest (-92%), Facebook (-72%), and YouTube (-64%).

- TikTok was the standout, growing 224% on AI Overviews and 313% on ChatGPT.

### How UGC citations are changing across AI platforms

Social media and UGC content appears to play a role in AI search, but the growth of that role isn’t the same on every AI platform. Google’s AI Overviews increased citations to nearly every UGC site we tracked, especially from video-based sites like YouTube and TikTok. Meanwhile, ChatGPT’s most-cited UGC source is Reddit, but even Reddit’s citations on ChatGPT decreased over 20% during the period studied.

Comparing these citations across the AI platforms shows which ones lean on UGC more when generating answers, and which sites are driving the change.

### 1. UGC citation trends diverge between ChatGPT and Google

Google is increasingly citing UGC and social media domains across all three of its AI products.

Combined monthly citations across AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Gemini rose from 75.8 million in January to 115.5 million in August — a 52% increase.

All three of Google AI platforms we tracked saw an increase in UGC citations:

- AI Overviews : +63%

- AI Mode : +32%

- Gemini : +84%

Google’s combined UGC citations rose through the spring and remained above 109 million a month since April.

Meanwhile, ChatGPT moved in the opposite direction.

In January, it gave UGC sites 18 million citations, and 21.2 million in February. However, that is where citations peaked, and in August they had fallen to 10.1 million.

That’s a 44% decrease, the opposite trend to what happened on Google’s AI platforms.

### 2. YouTube led Google’s AI citations, while Reddit dominated ChatGPT

Reddit and YouTube were the two most cited UGC sources in the set we tracked. However, their relative weight varied widely from platform to platform.

- AI Overviews: YouTube ranked first with 238 million citations, accounting for 45% of all tracked citations. While Reddit ranked second, its total citations amounted to less than half of YouTube's.

- AI Mode: YouTube again ranked first with 79.9 million citations. Reddit ranked third here, behind Facebook.

- Gemini: Reddit and YouTube are evenly split, with roughly 8.9 million citations each, together making up almost 88% of all citations.

- ChatGPT: Reddit led with 64.6 million citations — an almost 52% share of tracked UGC citations. YouTube ranked third behind Facebook, with 18.3 million — well less than a third of Reddit's citations.

This can help you decide where to start. If you want to target AI Overviews, prioritize YouTube over Reddit, since more people search on Google than ChatGPT. Partner with YouTube creators in your space, and use your own channel to cover the product details you want AI answers to cite.

If ChatGPT visibility is your main focus, start with Reddit. Track how your brand and category come up in discussions there

### 3. Several major UGC sites moved in opposite directions on AI Overviews and ChatGPT

For several of the largest tracked domains, monthly citation volume moved in opposite directions on AI Overviews and ChatGPT.

Pinterest showed the most dramatic split. Its monthly AI Overview citations increased 131% while its ChatGPT monthly citations plummeted 92%.

So, before you deprioritize a UGC platform because of a drop in AI citations, check which AI platform the drop came from. Pinterest nearly disappeared from ChatGPT citations, but it gained ground in AI Overviews over the same period.

### 4. Medium and Substack followed very different citation trends across AI platforms

Medium and Substack are both text-focused publishing platforms, but their citation trends looked very different.

Medium's monthly ChatGPT citations dropped 93%, while also dropping 17% on AI Mode, and just 3% on Gemini. On AI Overviews, Medium’s citations increased, but only by 6%.

Substack was much steadier.

Its monthly citations increased 68% on AI Overviews, 7% on AI Mode, and 3% on Gemini. On ChatGPT, they fell, but only by 9%, from around 62,000 in January to 56,000 in August.

If you’re considering publishing text on Medium or Substack for AI visibility, check whether those domains still get cited across your topics. In our data, Substack was the steadier option for long-form content, gaining on all three Google platforms and staying close to flat on ChatGPT.

### 5. TikTok increased citations across all four AI platforms

TikTok was one of the few major UGC sites to gain visibility across every AI platform we tracked.

Its monthly citation volume increased substantially from January to August:

- AI Overviews: +224%, from around 777,000 to 2.5 million

- AI Mode: +146%, from around 660,000 to 1.6 million

- ChatGPT: +313%, from roughly 45,500 to 188,000

- Gemini: +294%, from around 2,000 to 7,900

Unlike many of the larger UGC sites in the study, TikTok didn't show a platform split. It finished August with more monthly citations than it had in January across all four platforms.

This suggests that partnering with TikTok creators can improve AI visibility across the board. A positive review of your product on TikTok can also shape how your brand shows up in Google’s AI answers and ChatGPT.

### 6. Video sources are gaining ground rapidly in AI Overviews

YouTube and TikTok weren’t the only video-heavy sites that gained visibility in AI Overviews during the period we tracked.

As a whole, monthly AI Overview citations of the video sources we tracked increased 74%, from 25.8 million in January to 44.9 million in August.

All six video-oriented UGC sites in the study gained AI Overview citations:

- YouTube: +55%

- TikTok: +224%

- Instagram: +218%

- Snapchat: +141%

- Vimeo: +75%

- Dailymotion: +412%

YouTube remained by far the largest video source with the greatest total increase in citations. TikTok and Instagram each more than tripled their monthly AI Overview citations, while even smaller video platforms finished August above their January levels.

Here are three ways marketing teams can capitalize on this:

- Build videos around specific buyer questions. A video answering one clear comparison, workflow, limitation, or use case is easier to map to a search than a generic brand or product video.

- Monitor third-party video coverage as well as your own. Reviews, creator videos, and independent demonstrations can be used by AI Overviews when describing your product.

- Study the videos AI Overviews already cite. Note which creators and formats, such as demos, comparisons, or setup walkthroughs, appear for your branded and category searches, and use that to decide what to create.

### 7. AI referral traffic remains very small

Aside from citations, AI referrals still appear as a very small share of traffic to the UGC sites we tracked.

In August, AI assistant referrals accounted for just 0.14% of sessions to the tracked sites, and Google AI Mode a mere 0.02%.

By contrast, direct traffic accounted for 76.7% of traffic and organic search for 15.8%.

This does not mean that UGC sites aren't important for AI visibility. Referral traffic only shows whether someone clicked on a cited source, not how that source shaped the AI answer. A Reddit thread, YouTube review, or comparison video can influence how a product is described in an AI Overview or ChatGPT answer without the user needing to click on anything.

For marketing teams, the best advice is to track the prompts and keywords where you want your brand to be seen, and monitor how third-party UGC sites get cited. Check how present these UGC platforms are across your target topics.

### Find the UGC sites that shape AI answers in your niche

The patterns in this study won't look the same for every brand or category. And you don’t need to pivot your strategy to build a program for each of these UGC sites.

A gardening brand might discover that YouTube and TikTok appear frequently around its target topics. Meanwhile, a software company might see more Reddit discussions, LinkedIn posts, or Substack articles getting cited.

### Final thoughts

Social media and UGC sources are gaining ground fastest in Google’s AI products, especially video platforms.

Start with the sites that show up most in your niche, and decide how you want your brand and products to be represented there. Partner with creators or build your own YouTube channel or Substack.

This could be a sign of where things are heading. In October 2026, Google published documentation for a UGC Fresh Data Program , which lets approved platforms send new posts and engagement data to Google directly instead of waiting to be crawled. While this program came after our study period, it suggests Google’s reliance on UGC sources is more likely to grow than fade.

For marketers, building a positive presence on UGC platforms can shape how your brand shows up in Google’s AI Overviews.

### Research Methodology

For this analysis, we tracked citations for 18 UGC and social media domains across Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, and ChatGPT, using Semrush U.S. prompt data from January through August 2026.

The 18 domains were YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, Quora, TikTok, LinkedIn, Medium, Pinterest, X, Substack, Dailymotion, Snapchat, Behance, Vimeo, Twitch, Bilibili, and Bluesky.

We measured:

- Total citations by AI product and domain across the eight-month period

- Monthly citation trends and percentage changes

- Each domain's share of citations within the tracked group

- Referral sessions attributed to AI assistants and Google AI Search

The citation analysis covered approximately 528.3 million citations from AI Overviews, 263.6 million from AI Mode, 125.1 million from ChatGPT, and 20.3 million from Gemini.

These numbers should be read with the following caveats:

- Shares are within the tracked group. Citation shares refer to the 18 tracked domains, not all citations. Changes in citation counts don't establish changes in UGC's share of all AI citations.

- Sources are grouped at the domain level. The analysis doesn't establish that every cited page contains user-generated content, or that every citation to a video platform draws on the video itself.

- Blended and share figures are calculated. Combined totals across AI products and platform shares within groups are calculated from the reported monthly figures.

- Citations and referrals cover different products. The AI referral traffic analysis includes AI assistants beyond the four products in the citation analysis, so referral and citation totals shouldn't be used to calculate a click-through rate.
