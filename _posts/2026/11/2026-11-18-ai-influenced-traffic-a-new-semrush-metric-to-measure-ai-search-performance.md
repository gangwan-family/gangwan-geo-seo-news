## AI-influenced traffic: A new Semrush metric to measure AI search performance

Last week, a coalition of brands and measurement leaders said AI search needs a measurement standard. Today, Semrush is shipping the first measurable layer of one: AI-influenced traffic, a new metric that estimates the website visits a brand earns from being mentioned or cited in AI answers.

The coalition is attacking a real problem. In a study we ran this past year, 49% of marketers picked the “impact of AI on pipeline or revenue” as the metric they struggled to measure the most. Visibility scores alone can’t close that gap. Traffic is the missing step between being seen in AI answers and growing revenue from a website.

AI-influenced traffic fills in that missing step, and it’s available in Semrush today.

"Understanding how a mention or citation in an LLM influences traffic takes a lot of the guesswork out of strategy. Building AI visibility takes time, money and resources that smaller clients often don’t have. With the new AI-influenced traffic metric, we can identify the targets worth spending that time on."

Natalka Antoniuk , Digital Marketing Lead at Appius and an early-access tester of AI-influenced traffic

### What AI-influenced traffic measures

When an AI tool answers someone's question and mentions a brand, there’s a chance that person will visit the brand's website later — not by clicking the AI-cited link, but by searching for the brand on Google or visiting their site directly.

With traditional analytics, those visits would be filed under “direct” or “organic search”’ and AI’s role never shows up.

The only way to capture that influence is to estimate it with a predictive model, so that’s what Semrush did.

AI-influenced traffic estimates how many of those visits are influenced by seeing your brand in an AI answer.

It’s not just an estimate of AI referral traffic. It measures the wider impact of how AI visibility influences traffic through any channel. That means:

- Direct traffic: someone gets a recommendation from ChatGPT and types in a brand’s URL to visit their site directly.

- Branded organic search: someone reads an AI answer, then searches your brand name on Google.

- AI referral clicks: someone clicks a citation link inside the AI answer.

### Why its important

If AI visibility is the first layer and revenue is the end goal, then traffic is the step in between. It’s also the step most marketers can’t see today. AI-influenced traffic measures that missing step and gives marketers a way to benchmark this stage across their competitors.

You can use it to:

- Track trends: see whether AI is sending you more visitors over time, and across which topics

- Benchmark competitors: see how other brands turn their AI visibility into visits

- Find gaps: compare AI-influenced and organic traffic by topic to see which topics to prioritize for AI visibility

Since Semrush calculates it with the same model for every domain and topic every month, it’s best used as a benchmark or comparison number. Each figure is an estimate, but the comparison is apples to apples.

The metric sits in Organic Rankings 2.0, next to organic traffic, mentions, and citations for every topic a brand competes on.

You see SEO and AI visibility performance in one report, measured across the same topics.

Try it today: Enter any domain into the new Organic Rankings 2.0 tool to check its AI-influenced traffic.

### How Semrush calculates it

The estimate comes from a predictive model that works in three steps:

- It learns from real behavior. The model is trained on aggregated web activity that connects brand mentions in AI answers to website visits (through any channel) within a 24-hour window.

- It looks at the brand’s AI visibility. It checks how often and how consistently a brand appears in AI answers across its topics, using AI responses from Semrush’s AI search database.

- It weighs topics by their demand. Topics that more people ask about have more weight in estimating traffic.

### Our principles of AI visibility data quality

Estimating traffic influence from AI answers is only as good as the AI visibility data behind it. AI responses can change from run to run, so a metric built on guessed prompts or single responses would be noise.

That’s why AI-influenced traffic is built on our three principles of AI visibility data quality :

- Demand led: Topics are built from real search demand in Semrush’s leading SEO database of 29.4B+ keywords. They don’t come from a list of guessed prompts.

- Topic structured: AI-influenced traffic is measured at the topic level, where related keywords and AI prompts are grouped into one unit. This way, you measure your traffic return across a whole category and not just a specific phrasing or keyword.

- Response validated: Visibility metrics come from 2.18B + observed AI responses, sampled repeatedly because AI answers change from one run to the next.
