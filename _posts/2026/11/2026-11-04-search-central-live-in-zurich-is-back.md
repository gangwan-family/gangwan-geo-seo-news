## Search Central Live in Zurich is back!

Wednesday, November 4, 2026

Search Central Live is coming to Zurich on December 4, 2026!

Our Search Central Live event series is a great way for local web creators, developers, SEO professionals, and site owners to connect with Googlers and each other. There will be sessions on Google Search, Shopping, Discover, Gemini and the always-popular Q&A session with the speakers.

### What to expect

Join us for a full day learning about new opportunities and developments in Search.

Sessions will cover recent updates in Search, insights into how Search features work, and practical guidance for site owners and SEOs. In addition to presentations and a live Q&A session, you'll have dedicated time for in-person networking. The event won't be recorded or live-streamed.

### Call for Community Speakers

We want to highlight voices from the community! We're opening a call for community speakers to share their insights, case studies, or practical experiences. If you have a topic or story you'd like to share on stage, let us know in the call for speakers form .

### How to join

Admission is free, but you must apply in advance and space is limited.

Apply for an invitation .

Accepted attendees will receive a confirmation email with full details, which you'll need to show at the entrance.

See you in Zurich!
