## ChatGPT Enterprise now available in AWS Marketplace

View in AWS Marketplace (opens in a new window)

Loading…

Today, ChatGPT Enterprise is available through AWS Marketplace. The listing gives organizations a simpler way to discover, buy, and manage ChatGPT Enterprise using their existing AWS purchasing and billing relationship and to apply eligible spend toward their AWS commitments.

More than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week . ChatGPT Enterprise brings that familiar experience to work with ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, Codex, and dots. With ChatGPT Enterprise now available in AWS Marketplace, AWS customers get the same models, features, and functionality available directly from OpenAI, with new features released at the same time.

These OpenAI products complement the OpenAI models available in Amazon Bedrock . Developers can build AI applications and agents with OpenAI models in Amazon Bedrock, while teams across the business use ChatGPT Enterprise in their daily work. Organizations can choose the approach that fits their workloads and governance requirements.

“ Enterprises want to put AI to work across their teams without adding new layers of procurement. With ChatGPT Enterprise in AWS Marketplace, customers can buy it through their AWS account, govern it alongside the rest of their software spend, and move from evaluation to rollout faster. Working with OpenAI, we’re bringing one of the world’s most widely used AI products to AWS customers. ”

—Matt Yanchyshyn, Vice President, AWS Marketplace and Partner Services, AWS

“ Enterprises increasingly rely on both AWS and OpenAI to put AI to work, and our partnership is about making that combination stronger for them. Joint customers can now bring ChatGPT to their teams through the cloud provider they already trust. Together with AWS, we’re simplifying the path to adoption and helping more organizations unlock what’s possible with AI at scale. ”

—Chris Grusz, Managing Director, Cloud Partnerships, OpenAI

“ Bringing ChatGPT Enterprise to AWS Marketplace gives companies like Sophos a simpler path to expand AI across our teams. Having the option to buy through a single channel and apply existing AWS commitments removes friction that often slows enterprise AI adoption. Time that would otherwise go to procurement can go toward using AI to deliver more value for our customers. ”

—John Peterson, Chief Technology Officer, Sophos
