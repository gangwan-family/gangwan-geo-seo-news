## New learning paths: curated YouTube playlists for Search fundamentals and Technical SEO

Tuesday, October 6, 2026

Over the years, the Search Central YouTube channel has published hundreds of videos across various series, from Search for Beginners and Ask Googlebot to Search Console Training and Search Off the Record . While our series structure works well for deep dives and episodic updates, we know that finding a clear, sequential learning path from scratch can sometimes be challenging.

Whether you're just starting out or managing complex technical infrastructure at scale, we've organized our content into two dedicated playlists to guide your journey.

### Search fundamentals for beginners

If you're a website owner, marketer, content creator, or new to SEO, this playlist is designed to take you from core concepts to practical measurement.

You'll learn how Google crawls, indexes, and serves content, how to optimize your presence in search results, and how to measure performance using Google Search Console and Google Trends.

Topics covered include:

- How Search works: An introduction to crawling, indexing, and serving, plus an anatomy of search results.

- Getting started with Search Console: Understanding your setup, analyzing performance reports, and discovering Search Console Insights.

- Market & content Insights: Practical guides to using Google Trends for SEO and identifying trending topics.

Watch the Search fundamentals for beginners playlist

### Technical SEO fundamentals

For developers and technical SEO specialists, this playlist focuses on the underlying infrastructure of Google Search.

We skip the high-level basics to focus on site health, crawl budget optimization, rendering considerations, and large-scale debugging.

Topics covered include:

- Crawl directives & architecture: Deep dives into robots.txt, crawl stats reports, and managing crawl budget.

- Indexing & debugging at scale: Troubleshooting indexation issues, using the URL inspection tool effectively, and duplicate content management.

- Serving, rendering & APIs: Understanding how browsers parse HTML for SEO, page weight considerations, and automating workflows with Search Console bulk data exports and APIs.

Watch the Technical SEO fundamentals playlist

Both playlists are now live on the Search Central channel playlists . Save them to your library, share them with your team, and subscribe for upcoming tutorials and deep dives.
